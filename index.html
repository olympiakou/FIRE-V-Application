FIRE-V · About the map

FIRE-V is a research prototype designed to visualise vegetation change and post-fire impacts using openly available satellite data.

Satellite observations: 05 June, 07 July, 27 July and 04 August 2026. Fire event: 29 July 2026, Rethymno, Crete.

Map interpretation: NDVI describes relative greenness; NDMI is a spectral moisture indicator and does not independently diagnose drought. dNBR describes change in surface spectral response after fire. Severity classes are illustrative and not field-validated damage estimates.

Data and limitations: Sentinel-2 Level-2A; CORINE Land Cover 2018 (minimum mapping unit 25 ha). Mapped fire-affected area was derived using dNBR ≥ 0.10 and visual comparison, not an independently verified fire perimeter. The earlier satellite processing used an SCL 4/5 mask, potentially excluding some burned pixels. Land-cover overlay is indicative of 2018 mapping, not parcel-level 2026 inventory.

Area figures represent satellite-derived map estimates and are not verified assessments of agricultural losses.
